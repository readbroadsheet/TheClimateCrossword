The Climate Crossword

This is a place to explore climate thematically, through a puzzle structure that's been around for over a century. Crosswords are built on deductive reasoning, lateral thinking, and error recognition. The Climate Crossword applies that structure to climate as subject matter, with an additional anagram layer, so each puzzle works on two levels instead of one. A set of marked cells across the grid collect letters as they're solved, and those letters rearrange into a second, hidden answer once enough of the puzzle is filled in. Each puzzle is published as a numbered issue and stays archived permanently, so past issues remain solvable rather than being replaced by newer ones.
