How to play
  • Click a clue in the Across or Down list and scroll to fill it in the grid by typing the letter.
  • At a crossing cell (where two words meet), clicking that cell highlights both possible clues — click the one you want to solve.
  • Type letters directly; the cursor moves forward automatically. Backspace or Delete removes a letter and steps back if the current cell is already empty.
  • When a word is fully typed, it's checked automatically: correct flashes green and locks in permanently; incorrect flashes red and clears.
  • Marked cells in blue feed the Meta Puzzle below. Solving a word reveals its letter there, in the order you solve them.
  • Once all marked cells are revealed, unscramble them into the hidden word and submit.
  • Reset (bottom of page) clears everything, with a confirmation first.
